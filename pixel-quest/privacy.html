Pixel Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Juegabit, a brand operated by ITCSUY SRL ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in Uruguay, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Pixel Quest mobile game (the "Game"). By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

The data controller is ITCSUY SRL (operating as Juegabit), Uruguay. For any privacy question or request, contact us at pablo.caviglia@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. What we collect depends on how you play:

DataWhenWhy
Name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier Only if you choose to Sign in with Google To create and secure your account and sync your progress
Game progress and preferences (completed pictures, settings, in-game state) While you play To save your progress and restore it across sessions and devices
A randomly generated anonymous identifier If you play as a guest (no sign-in) To store progress locally/anonymously without identifying you
Basic technical data needed to operate the service (e.g. connection/request data) When the app communicates with our backend To deliver content, authenticate requests, and keep the service reliable and secure
Usage & diagnostics data (in-app events such as screens viewed and pictures opened/completed, an app-instance identifier, and crash diagnostics) While you play To understand which features are used and to detect and fix crashes, so we can improve the Game. We use Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics for this, and we do not use it for advertising

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers for advertising. The Game does not show third-party ads and we do not sell your personal data.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the Game and save and sync your progress.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To operate, maintain, debug, and improve the Game.
  • To respond to your support or privacy requests.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data on the basis of: contract (to provide the Game you requested), consent (e.g. when you choose to sign in with Google), and our legitimate interests (to keep the service secure and working). You may withdraw consent at any time by signing out and/or requesting deletion.

5. Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) with us in accordance with the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. We use this only to create your account and sync progress. We do not request access to any other Google data. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Service providers & data sharing

We share data only with providers that help us run the Game, under appropriate safeguards:

  • Supabase — our backend and authentication provider, which hosts your account and game-progress data on our behalf.
  • Google — identity provider for "Sign in with Google".
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — product analytics and crash reporting, on our behalf, to help us understand usage and improve the Game.

We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

7. International data transfers

Our providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including outside Uruguay and the European Economic Area. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate legal safeguards.

8. Data retention

We keep account and progress data for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the Game. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize the associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights — including deleting your account and data — email pablo.caviglia@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also delete your account directly: see Delete your account.

10. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls through our backend provider. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may be announced in the Game.
